# C6 historical fixture

The adversarial synthetic world uses six symbols and three research dates:

| Date | Universe | Expected top-two decision |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-01-01 | AAA, BBB, CCC, DDD | AAA, BBB |
| 2025-04-01 | AAA, BBB, DDD, EEE | AAA, EEE |
| 2025-07-01 | AAA, DDD, EEE, FFF | FFF, AAA |

EEE physically exists in the price fixture before its entry date but is not
eligible at T0. FFF physically exists throughout the fixture but is not
eligible until T2. The expected ranking is supplied as persisted research
results, not generated by the backtester. C6 only simulates those frozen
decisions.

The holding policy is rebalance-date based: leaving a universe between dates
does not cause an off-cycle liquidation. At the next rebalance the next
persisted ResearchRun determines the target portfolio.
